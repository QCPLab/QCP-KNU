Research

Our work follows a drug from the dose to the concentration in the body: how it is absorbed, metabolized, transported and eliminated, and what changes that in disease, with other drugs and between individuals.

  • Pharmacokinetics of drug candidates and disease models
  • Drug metabolism and metabolites
  • Drug transporters and drug–drug interactions
  • Quantitative modeling and prediction
  • Bioanalysis
  • Department topics

Pharmacokinetics of drug candidates and disease models

We characterize the systemic exposure of new drug candidates and marketed drugs, in healthy animals and in disease models, and identify what governs it.

Approaches
In vivo PK · Disease models · Drug candidates

Drug metabolism and metabolites

We study how drugs are metabolized, which metabolites form, and how metabolism shapes exposure and safety, supported by LC-MS/MS metabolite analysis.

Approaches
Metabolite profiling · In vitro metabolism · LC-MS/MS

Selected publications

  1. 2015

    The identification of lobeglitazone metabolites in rat liver microsomes and the kinetics of the in vivo formation of the major metabolite M1 in rats

    Journal of Pharmaceutical and Biomedical Analysis

  2. 2018

    Metabolic and pharmacokinetic characterization of a new synthetic cannabinoid APINAC in rats

    Forensic Toxicology

  3. 2015

    Gender differences in the hepatic elimination and pharmacokinetics of lobeglitazone in rats

    Biopharmaceutics & Drug Disposition

Drug transporters and drug–drug interactions

Co-prescribed drugs often compete for the same enzymes and transporters. We examine enzyme- and transporter-mediated interactions to explain changes in efficacy and adverse effects.

Approaches
Transporters · Enzymes · Interaction risk

Selected publications

  1. 2010

    Sitagliptin attenuates metformin-mediated AMPK phosphorylation through inhibition of organic cation transporters

    Xenobiotica

  2. 2020

    Recent trends of metabolism-based drug interaction research

    Yakhak Hoeji

Quantitative modeling and prediction

We use pharmacokinetic, physiologically based (PBPK) and population (nonlinear mixed-effects) models, and machine learning, to interpret data and predict exposure in situations that were not tested.

Approaches
Compartmental · Population (NLME) · PBPK · Machine learning

Selected publications

  1. 2025

    Understanding the Crossing of Blood-Brain Barrier Using Nanocarriers: Current Trends and the Role of Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Modeling

    IEEE Transactions on NanoBioscience

  2. 2013

    Pharmacokinetic characterization of the novel TAZ modulator TM-25659 using a multicompartment kinetic model in rats and a possibility of its drug-drug interactions in humans

    Xenobiotica

Bioanalysis: the measurement behind every study

Every pharmacokinetic result starts with a reliable concentration. We develop and validate LC-MS/MS methods to measure drugs and metabolites in biological samples such as plasma.

Selected publications

    Journal of Chromatography B

  3. 2014

    Quantitative determination of enzalutamide, an anti-prostate cancer drug, in rat plasma using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry, and its application to a pharmacokinetic study

    Biomedical Chromatography
